## Full description (Google Play / App Store)

**Anything can be the answer. Can you guess it?**

GUESS IT is the universal guessing game. One tap drops you into a mystery — a Bollywood
superstar, a blockbuster movie, a superhero, a secret 5-digit code, a number from the real
world — and your job is to reason your way to the answer before your guesses run out.

**🔍 CLUE GUESS** — Clues appear one at a time, vague first. Guess early, score big.
**🔢 CRACK THE CODE** — A secret 5-digit code. Every guess tells you what's exact, what's
misplaced, what's a miss. Pure deduction.
**⬆️ HIGHER / LOWER** — Real-world numbers, shrinking ranges, tight attempts.
**❓ QUICK PICK** — Four options, one ticking timer. Faster = bigger score.

**🤖 BATTLE AI MINDS** — Race the Detective, the Calculator or the Machine, from Rookie to
Hard. They think in real time, take clues like you do — and after the game, you can see
exactly how they solved it.

**🌎 RACE YOUR FRIENDS LIVE** — Two phones, one puzzle, real time. Make a room, share the
code, watch each other's progress. First to crack it wins.

**⚔️ CHALLENGE ANYONE** — Finish a game, send the link. Your friend plays the exact same
puzzle. Winner takes the bragging rights.

**👥 PASS & PLAY** — One phone, up to four players, alternating guesses. Party mode.

**🎯 DAILY MYSTERY** — One shared puzzle for the whole world, every day. Keep your streak
alive.

Plus XP, levels, achievements, streaks and a scoring system that rewards nerve — every extra
clue costs you points.

✓ Free, no ads, no sign-up
✓ No data collected — your progress stays on your device
✓ 2-minute games, endless variety

Think. Guess. Outsmart.
